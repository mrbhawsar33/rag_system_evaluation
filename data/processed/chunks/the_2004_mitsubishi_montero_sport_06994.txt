The 2004 Mitsubishi Montero Sport is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 197 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with rear drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 15 city MPG and 19 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 436.
