The 2011 Mazda Tribute is a compact 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder flex-fuel (unleaded/e85) engine producing 240 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, flex fuel.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 19 city MPG and 25 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 586.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $27,315.
