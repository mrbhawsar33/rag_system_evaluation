The 1993 Mazda Navajo is a compact 2dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 160 horsepower.
The transmission is manual with rear drive.
It has 2 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 21 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 586.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $2,000.
