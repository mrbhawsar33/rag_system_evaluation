The 2008 Volkswagen Touareg 2 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 10-cylinder diesel engine producing 310 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, diesel.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 15 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 873.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $68,340.
