The 2016 Ford Explorer is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder flex-fuel (unleaded/e85) engine producing 290 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, flex fuel.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 23 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 5657.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $33,050.
