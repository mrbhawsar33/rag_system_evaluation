The 2015 Lexus NX 300h is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 194 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, hybrid.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 35 city MPG and 31 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 454.
