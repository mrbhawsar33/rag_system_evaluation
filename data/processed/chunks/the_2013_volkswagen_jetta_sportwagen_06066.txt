The 2013 Volkswagen Jetta SportWagen is a compact wagon.
It features a 5-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 170 horsepower.

It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 24 city MPG and 31 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 873.
