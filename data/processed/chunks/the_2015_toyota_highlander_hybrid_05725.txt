The 2015 Toyota Highlander Hybrid is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 280 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, hybrid.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 27 city MPG and 28 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2031.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $47,850.
