The 2016 Toyota 4Runner is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 270 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with rear drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 22 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2031.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $34,010.
