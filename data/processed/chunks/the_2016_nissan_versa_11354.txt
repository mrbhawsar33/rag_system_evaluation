The 2016 Nissan Versa is a compact sedan.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 109 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 31 city MPG and 40 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $15,580.
