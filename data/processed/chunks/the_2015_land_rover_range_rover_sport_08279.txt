The 2015 Land Rover Range Rover Sport is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder flex-fuel (premium unleaded required/e85) engine producing 340 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: flex fuel, luxury, performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 23 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 258.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $68,295.
