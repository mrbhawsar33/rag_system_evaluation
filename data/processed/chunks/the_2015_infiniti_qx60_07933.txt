The 2015 Infiniti QX60 is a large 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 230 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, hybrid.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 26 city MPG and 28 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 190.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $45,400.
