The 2016 Porsche Cayenne is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 8-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 570 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, high-performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 21 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1715.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $157,300.
