The 2002 Oldsmobile Silhouette is a midsize passenger minivan.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 185 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 22 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 26.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $35,970.
