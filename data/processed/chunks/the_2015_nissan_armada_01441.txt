The 2015 Nissan Armada is a large 4dr suv.
It features a 8-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 317 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 12 city MPG and 18 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $46,500.
