The 2014 Mazda 5 is a compact passenger minivan.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 157 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 22 city MPG and 28 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 586.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $24,670.
