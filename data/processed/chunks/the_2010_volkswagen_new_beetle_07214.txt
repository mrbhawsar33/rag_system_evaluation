The 2010 Volkswagen New Beetle is a compact convertible.
It features a 5-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 150 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 2 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 20 city MPG and 28 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 873.
