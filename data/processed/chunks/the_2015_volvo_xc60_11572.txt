The 2015 Volvo XC60 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 302 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 22 city MPG and 30 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 870.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $44,050.
