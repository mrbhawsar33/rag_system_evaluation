The 2017 Nissan Rogue is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 170 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 25 city MPG and 32 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $31,310.
