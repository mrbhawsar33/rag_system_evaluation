The 2016 Volvo XC90 is a large 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 250 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 22 city MPG and 26 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 870.
