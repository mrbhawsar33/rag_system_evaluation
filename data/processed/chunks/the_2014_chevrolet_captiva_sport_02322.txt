The 2014 Chevrolet Captiva Sport is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder flex-fuel (unleaded/e85) engine producing 180 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, flex fuel.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 20 city MPG and 28 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1385.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $25,145.
