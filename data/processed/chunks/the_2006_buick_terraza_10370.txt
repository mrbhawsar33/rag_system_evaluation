The 2006 Buick Terraza is a midsize passenger minivan.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 196 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 15 city MPG and 21 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 155.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $33,250.
