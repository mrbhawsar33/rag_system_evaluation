The 2017 Kia Sorento is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 290 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 18 city MPG and 25 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1720.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $28,990.
