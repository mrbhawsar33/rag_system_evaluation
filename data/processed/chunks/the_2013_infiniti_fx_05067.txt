The 2013 Infiniti FX is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 8-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 390 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, high-performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 190.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $61,500.
