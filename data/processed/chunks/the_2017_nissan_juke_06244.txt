The 2017 Nissan Juke is a compact 4dr hatchback.
It features a 4-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 188 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, hatchback.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 28 city MPG and 32 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $20,250.
