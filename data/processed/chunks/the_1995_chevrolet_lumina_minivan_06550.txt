The 1995 Chevrolet Lumina Minivan is a compact cargo minivan.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 120 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 3 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 21 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1385.
