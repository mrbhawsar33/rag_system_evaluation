The 2014 Nissan Quest is a compact passenger minivan.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 260 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 19 city MPG and 25 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $42,870.
