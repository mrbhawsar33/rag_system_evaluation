The 2009 Infiniti EX35 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 297 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 23 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 190.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $37,400.
