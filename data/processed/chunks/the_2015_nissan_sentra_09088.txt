The 2015 Nissan Sentra is a midsize sedan.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 130 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 30 city MPG and 40 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $17,780.
