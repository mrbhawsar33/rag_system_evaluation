The 2009 Chevrolet TrailBlazer is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 285 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1385.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $31,330.
