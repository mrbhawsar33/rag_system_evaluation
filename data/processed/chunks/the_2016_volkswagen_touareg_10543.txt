The 2016 Volkswagen Touareg is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 280 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 23 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 873.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $42,705.
