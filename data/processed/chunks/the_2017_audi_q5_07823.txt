The 2017 Audi Q5 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder flex-fuel (premium unleaded recommended/e85) engine producing 220 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, flex fuel, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 20 city MPG and 27 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 3105.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $40,900.
