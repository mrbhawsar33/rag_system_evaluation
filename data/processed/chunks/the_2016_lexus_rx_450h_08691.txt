The 2016 Lexus RX 450h is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 308 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, hybrid.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 30 city MPG and 28 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 454.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $53,635.
