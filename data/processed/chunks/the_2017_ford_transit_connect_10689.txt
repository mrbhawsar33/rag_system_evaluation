The 2017 Ford Transit Connect is a compact cargo minivan.
It features a 4-cylinder flex-fuel (unleaded/e85) engine producing 169 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 20 city MPG and 27 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 5657.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $23,900.
