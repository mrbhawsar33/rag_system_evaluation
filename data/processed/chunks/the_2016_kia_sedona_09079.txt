The 2016 Kia Sedona is a midsize passenger minivan.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 276 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 22 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1720.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $39,900.
