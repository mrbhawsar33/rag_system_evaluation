The 2016 Volvo XC60 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 302 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 20 city MPG and 28 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 870.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $51,300.
