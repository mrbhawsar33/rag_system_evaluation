The 2012 Mazda CX-7 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 161 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 20 city MPG and 27 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 586.
