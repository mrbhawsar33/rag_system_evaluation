The 2010 Chevrolet Avalanche is a large crew cab pickup.
It features a 8-cylinder flex-fuel (unleaded/e85) engine producing 320 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with rear drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, flex fuel.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 15 city MPG and 21 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1385.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $45,815.
