The 2007 Buick Rainier is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 291 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 155.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $33,570.
