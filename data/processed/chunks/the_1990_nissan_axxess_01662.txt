The 1990 Nissan Axxess is a compact passenger minivan.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 138 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 3 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $2,000.
