The 2006 Infiniti FX45 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 8-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 320 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 13 city MPG and 17 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 190.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $49,750.
