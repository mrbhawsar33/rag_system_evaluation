The 2015 Lexus RX 350 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 270 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 18 city MPG and 26 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 454.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $50,220.
