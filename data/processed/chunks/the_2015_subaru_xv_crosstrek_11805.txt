The 2015 Subaru XV Crosstrek is a compact 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 160 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, hybrid.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 30 city MPG and 34 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 640.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $25,995.
