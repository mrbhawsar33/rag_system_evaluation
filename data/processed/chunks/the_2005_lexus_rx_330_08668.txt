The 2005 Lexus RX 330 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 230 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 22 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 454.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $37,425.
