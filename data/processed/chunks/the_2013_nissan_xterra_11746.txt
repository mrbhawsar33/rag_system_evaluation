The 2013 Nissan Xterra is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 261 horsepower.
The transmission is manual with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $25,850.
