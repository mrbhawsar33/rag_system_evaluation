The 2017 Nissan Armada is a large 4dr suv.
It features a 8-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 390 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with rear drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 19 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $57,590.
