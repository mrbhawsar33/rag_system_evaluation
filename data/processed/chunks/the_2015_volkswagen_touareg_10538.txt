The 2015 Volkswagen Touareg is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 380 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, hybrid.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 20 city MPG and 24 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 873.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $66,995.
