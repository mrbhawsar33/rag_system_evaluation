The 2015 Nissan Murano is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 260 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 21 city MPG and 28 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $38,550.
