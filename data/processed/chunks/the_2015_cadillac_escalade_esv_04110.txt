The 2015 Cadillac Escalade ESV is a large 4dr suv.
It features a 8-cylinder flex-fuel (unleaded/e85) engine producing 420 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: flex fuel, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1624.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $78,570.
