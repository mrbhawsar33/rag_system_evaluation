The 2015 Nissan NV200 is a compact cargo minivan.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 131 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 24 city MPG and 26 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $21,710.
