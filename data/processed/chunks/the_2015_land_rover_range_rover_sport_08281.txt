The 2015 Land Rover Range Rover Sport is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 8-cylinder flex-fuel (premium unleaded required/e85) engine producing 550 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: flex fuel, factory tuner, luxury, high-performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 19 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 258.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $110,475.
