The 2014 Volkswagen Jetta SportWagen is a compact wagon.
It features a 5-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 170 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 23 city MPG and 30 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 873.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $26,685.
