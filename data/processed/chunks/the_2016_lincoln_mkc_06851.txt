The 2016 Lincoln MKC is a compact 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 240 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 19 city MPG and 26 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 61.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $39,585.
