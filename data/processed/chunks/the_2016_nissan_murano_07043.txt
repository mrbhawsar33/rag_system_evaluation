The 2016 Nissan Murano is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 250 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, hybrid.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 28 city MPG and 31 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $42,180.
