The 2015 Porsche Cayenne is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder diesel engine producing 240 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, diesel.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 20 city MPG and 29 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1715.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $61,700.
