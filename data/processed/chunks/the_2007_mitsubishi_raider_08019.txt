The 2007 Mitsubishi Raider is a compact extended cab pickup.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 210 horsepower.
The transmission is manual with rear drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 436.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $19,890.
