The 2015 Nissan Frontier is a compact crew cab pickup.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 261 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 15 city MPG and 21 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $32,560.
