The 2015 Volvo XC60 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 240 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 24 city MPG and 31 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 870.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $38,850.
