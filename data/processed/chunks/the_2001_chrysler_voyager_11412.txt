The 2001 Chrysler Voyager is a midsize passenger minivan.
It features a 6-cylinder flex-fuel (unleaded/e85) engine producing 180 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 22 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1013.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $23,525.
