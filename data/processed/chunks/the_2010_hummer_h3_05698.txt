The 2010 HUMMER H3 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 8-cylinder flex-fuel (unleaded/e85) engine producing 300 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: flex fuel, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 13 city MPG and 16 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 130.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $41,705.
