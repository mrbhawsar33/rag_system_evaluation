The 2012 Nissan Murano CrossCabriolet is a midsize convertible suv.
It features a 6-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 265 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 2 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 22 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 2009.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $44,540.
