The 2015 Bentley Continental GT is a midsize convertible.
It features a 12-cylinder flex-fuel (premium unleaded required/e85) engine producing 567 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 2 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: exotic, flex fuel, luxury, high-performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 12 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 520.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $223,600.
