The 2015 Infiniti QX60 is a large 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 265 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 21 city MPG and 27 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 190.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $42,400.
