The 2011 Suzuki Equator is a compact extended cab pickup.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 261 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with four drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 14 city MPG and 19 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 481.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $25,979.
