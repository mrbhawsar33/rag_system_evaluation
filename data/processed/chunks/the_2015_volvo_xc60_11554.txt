The 2015 Volvo XC60 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 300 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 24 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 870.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $48,250.
