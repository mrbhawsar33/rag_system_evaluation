The 2000 Plymouth Voyager is a midsize passenger minivan.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 150 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 3 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 18 city MPG and 24 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 535.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $2,008.
