The 2015 Ford Transit Connect is a compact cargo minivan.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 169 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 21 city MPG and 29 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 5657.
