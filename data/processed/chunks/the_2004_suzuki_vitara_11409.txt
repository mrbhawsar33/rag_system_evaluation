The 2004 Suzuki Vitara is a compact 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 165 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with rear drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 20 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 481.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $17,999.
