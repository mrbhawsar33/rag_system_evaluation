The 2015 Mazda CX-5 is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 184 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 25 city MPG and 32 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 586.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $27,970.
