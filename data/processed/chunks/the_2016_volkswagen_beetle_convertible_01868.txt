The 2016 Volkswagen Beetle Convertible is a compact convertible.
It features a 4-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 170 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 2 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 25 city MPG and 34 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 873.
