The 2008 Hyundai Entourage is a midsize passenger minivan.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 250 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 16 city MPG and 23 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1439.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $29,895.
