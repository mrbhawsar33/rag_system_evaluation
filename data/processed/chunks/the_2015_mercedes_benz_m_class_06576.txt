The 2015 Mercedes-Benz M-Class is a midsize 4dr suv.
It features a 6-cylinder premium unleaded engine producing 329 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with all drive.
It has 4 doors.
This model falls under the following categories: crossover, luxury, performance.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 18 city MPG and 22 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 617.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $62,900.
