The 2003 Chrysler Voyager is a midsize passenger minivan.
It features a 6-cylinder regular unleaded engine producing 180 horsepower.
The transmission is automatic with front drive.
It has 4 doors.
Fuel efficiency is rated at 17 city MPG and 24 highway MPG.
It has a popularity score of 1013.
The MSRP for this vehicle is $23,790.
